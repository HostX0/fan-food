# Iraqi menu photo and copy release

## Scope and provenance

All 58 existing product identities, prices, 73 variant options and eight categories are retained.
59 independent generated food photographs replace the mixed previous photo set: one per product,
plus a chicken-specific alternative for the pasta choice. The 480px and 960px WebP versions are
local repository assets, not expiring external hotlinks. The generated photo gallery was visually
reviewed before release: a consistent ivory stone table, sage cloth, cream serving ware, soft light
and similar camera angle. The gallery manifest records original generator job IDs and checksums.
Generated images illustrate dish identity, not the exact restaurant recipe, portion count or plating.

The customer-provided first two references define p020 (round kubba in tangy red broth with chard
and turnip), and the third defines p018 (pale round flattened jareesh discs, minced-meat filling).
p007 is mixed Iraqi biryani, p008 is half a chicken, p009 a whole chicken, and p014 is lamb quzi.
The hamburger products are sold by weight and are pictured as patties, not included bun meals.
The modal and cart now change the pasta photo for the selected chicken/meat option.

## Ingredient copy limits

Short descriptions use the dish name, supplied photos and commonly documented basic components.
They are NOT a certified full ingredient or allergen list. A visible expandable menu note states this.
House fillings are intentionally not invented. Products p021, p026, p039, p045, p046, p048, p055,
p057 and p058 carry an extra restaurant-confirmation note for the filling, assortment or house recipe.
The displayed bechamel option explicitly remains meat OR chicken; no choices or prices were invented.
No recipe is labelled allergen-free, vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free.

## Research approach

Each catalog name was searched with Iraqi-style qualifiers. Some house product names and the exact
biscuit dessert had no independently verifiable recipe; their presentation is based on the supplied
menu references and their uncertain details are marked for restaurant confirmation.
Online photographs informed food identification only: no third-party photo was copied into the site.

## UX kept intact

The six-item opening selection is editorial (not fabricated sales analytics), followed by all eight
original categories. Single-choice quick pills still decrement one unit down to zero. Custom item
notes, sizes, pasta choice, cart/favorite persistence, budget/Arabic search, Baghdad-only validation,
Iraqi phone handling, explicit location confirmation and Waze/WhatsApp composition are preserved.
The checkout does not send an order until the customer presses Send inside WhatsApp.
Customer contact details and coordinates are not added to persistent client storage.

The new tests cover descriptions on all cards, local responsive assets, the corrected kubba types,
pasta choice photos, readable small-screen copy and overflow. Existing regression suites remain.
